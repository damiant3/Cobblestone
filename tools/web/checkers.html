How to Play Checkers

Goal: Capture all of your opponent's pieces or block them so they can't move.

Movement: Pieces move diagonally forward one square. Kings can move diagonally in any direction.

Captures: Jump over an adjacent opponent piece to an empty square beyond it. Multiple jumps are allowed in one turn.

Kings: When a piece reaches the opposite end of the board, it becomes a king (shown with a crown).

This simulation: AI plays both sides using minimax with depth-3 search.
