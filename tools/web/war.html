How to Play War

Goal: Win all 52 cards.

Setup: The deck is split evenly between two players (26 cards each).

Each round: Both players flip their top card. The higher card wins both cards. If tied, it's "war" — extra cards are wagered and the next flip decides.

Card ranks: 2 (low) through Ace (high). Suits don't matter.

End: The game ends when one player runs out of cards. The other player wins.
