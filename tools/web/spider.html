How to Play Spider Solitaire

Goal: Clear all 8 suits by building complete King-to-Ace descending sequences of the same suit.

Tableau: 10 columns of cards. Move cards between columns to build descending sequences. Only same-suit sequences can be moved as a group.

Stock: When stuck, deal one card to each column from the stock pile.

Completion: A complete K-A same-suit run is automatically removed. Clear all 8 to win.

This simulation: AI plays automatically, making moves and dealing from stock.
