How to Play Risk

Goal: Conquer all 12 territories. Last player standing wins.

Flow: Each turn, the current player deploys reinforcement armies, then attacks neighboring territories. Combat is dice-based: attacker and defender each roll, highest die wins.

Elimination: When a player loses all territories, they are eliminated.

This simulation: AI plays up to 100 turns with 4 commanders fighting over 12 territories.
