# Workspace artwork

`nordic-forge.png` was generated locally in Diffusion Forge with DreamShaper XL
Lightning, seed 260927, DPM++ SDE with Karras scheduling, six steps and CFG 2,
at 1344 by 576 pixels. The prompt requests a quiet Nordic meadow and fjord,
ancient tree, misty mountains, muted forest and slate colors, amber dawn light,
and space for a website banner, without people or lettering.

`valheim-logo.svg` is Valheim's official logo from the inline logo on
[the game's official website](https://www.valheimgame.com/), retrieved
2026-09-27. It identifies the selected game; the background is generated artwork.
The standalone workspace embeds both assets through `build-site.ps1`.
