Tic-Tac-Toe

The engine is Codex, the same source the bare-metal build runs, compiled to WebAssembly by the project's own wasm backend. It searches the game to the end on every move, so it cannot be beaten. A draw is a win.

Loading the engine…

What is running here. apps/games/classic/TicTacToe.codex and Minimax.codex hold the rules and the search. apps/games/build-wasm.ps1 compiles them to the compiler's inner language, hands that to codex/plugs/wasm, and the module your browser just loaded is what came back. No JavaScript decides a move.

Why the AI never loses. ttt-perfect-ai searches every line of play to the end and prefers the fastest win and the slowest loss. The bare-metal battery plays it against every legal reply: 92 games as X and 569 as O, zero losses in either. This module reproduces those counts exactly; apps/games/wasm-verify.mjs is the arm.
