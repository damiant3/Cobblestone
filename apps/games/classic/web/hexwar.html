How to Play Hex War

Scenario: Stalingrad. A hex-based wargame where two sides fight for control of the city.

Flow: Each turn, units move across the hex grid, attack enemy positions, and try to capture key objectives.

Combat: Units have attack and defense values. Terrain modifies combat outcomes. Supply lines matter.

Victory: The game runs for a set number of turns. Victory is determined by objectives held at the end.

This simulation: AI plays both sides. The progress bar shows how far into the battle we are.
