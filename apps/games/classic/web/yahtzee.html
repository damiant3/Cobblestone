How to Play Yahtzee

Dice: Roll 5 dice up to 3 times per turn, keeping any between rolls.

Scoring: After rolling, score in one of 13 categories: Ones through Sixes (sum of matching dice), Three/Four of a Kind, Full House, Small/Large Straight, Yahtzee (five of a kind), or Chance (sum of all dice).

Categories: Each category can only be used once per game.

End: After 13 turns (all categories filled), highest total score wins.
