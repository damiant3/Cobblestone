How to Play Mahjong Solitaire

Mahjong Solitaire (Shanghai). 144 tiles arranged in layers. Match and remove pairs of identical free tiles. A tile is free if it has no tile on top and at least one side (left or right) is open. Game ends when no more pairs can be found or all tiles are cleared.
