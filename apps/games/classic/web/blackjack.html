How to Play Blackjack

Goal: Get a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer without going over (busting).

Card values: Number cards = face value. Face cards (J/Q/K) = 10. Ace = 11 or 1.

Deal: Player and dealer each get 2 cards. Dealer's second card is face-down (the "hole card").

Player turn: Hit (take a card) or Stand. Bust if over 21.

Dealer turn: Hole card revealed. Dealer must hit until reaching 17+.

Result: Closest to 21 wins. Bust = automatic loss. Tie = push (no winner).
