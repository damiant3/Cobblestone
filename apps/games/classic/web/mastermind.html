How to Play Mastermind

Goal: Guess the secret 4-color code in 10 guesses or fewer.

Colors: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, Orange (6 colors, 4 positions).

Feedback: After each guess, you receive feedback pegs:

● Black peg = right color in the right position.
○ White peg = right color in the wrong position.

Strategy: The AI uses the remaining candidate pool to narrow down possibilities each guess.

This simulation: AI codebreaker tries to crack the secret in 10 guesses using elimination strategy.
