How to Play Dots and Boxes

Goal: Complete more boxes than your opponent on a 4x4 dot grid.

Play: Take turns drawing one line between adjacent dots. When you complete the 4th side of a box, you score it and go again.

Strategy: Avoid giving your opponent chains of boxes. Force them to open chains for you to claim.

End: Game ends when all 24 lines are drawn (9 boxes total). Most boxes wins.
