How to Play Go

Goal: Control more territory than your opponent on a 9x9 board.

Play: Players alternate placing stones. Black goes first. Stones are placed on intersections, not squares.

Capture: Surround an opponent's group (remove all its liberties) to capture it. Captured stones are removed from the board.

Ko rule: You cannot immediately recapture a stone that just captured yours (prevents infinite loops).

Passing: A player may pass instead of placing a stone. Two consecutive passes end the game.

Scoring: Count surrounded empty points (territory) + captured stones. Highest total wins.

Note: This AI sometimes passes early, resulting in short games.
