How to Play Connect Four

Goal: Be the first to connect four of your discs in a row -- horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Play: Players take turns dropping one disc into any column. The disc falls to the lowest available row.

Strategy: Block your opponent while building your own connections. Control the center column for maximum options.

This simulation: AI plays both sides, taking a win where there is one, blocking one where there is not, and otherwise favouring the centre columns.
