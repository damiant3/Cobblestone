How to Play Pinochle

Goal: Be the first team to reach a target score by winning tricks and forming melds.

The deck: Pinochle uses a special 48-card deck with two copies of each card from 9 through Ace in all four suits (no 2-8 cards). Card ranking from high to low: A, 10, K, Q, J, 9.

Teams: Four players in two partnerships of two. Partners sit across from each other.

Melds: After the deal, players score points for specific card combinations (melds) in their hand. Common melds include: Pinochle (Jack of Diamonds + Queen of Spades), Marriage (King + Queen of same suit), and Runs (A-10-K-Q-J of trump).

Trick-taking: After melding, players take turns playing cards. You must follow suit if possible; otherwise you must play trump if possible. The highest trump wins, or the highest card of the led suit if no trump is played.

Scoring: Points come from melds and from capturing certain cards in tricks (Aces=11, Tens=10, Kings=4, Queens=3, Jacks=2). Last trick is worth an extra 10 points.
