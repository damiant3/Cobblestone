How to Play Mancala

Mancala (Kalah variant). Two players, 6 pits each plus a store. On your turn, pick up all stones from one of your pits and sow them counter-clockwise, one per pit. If your last stone lands in your store, go again. If it lands in an empty pit on your side, capture that stone plus the opposite pit's stones. Game ends when one side is empty; remaining stones go to the other player's store. Most stones wins.
