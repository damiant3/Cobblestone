How to Play Conway's Life

Conway's Game of Life. Each cell is alive or dead. A dead cell with exactly 3 alive neighbors becomes alive. An alive cell with 2 or 3 alive neighbors survives, otherwise it dies. 20x20 toroidal grid (edges wrap). The AI seeds an initial pattern and steps through generations.
