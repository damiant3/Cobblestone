How to Play Bridge

Goal: Score more points than the opposing partnership over 8 hands.

Teams: North/South vs East/West.

Bidding: Teams bid to set a contract -- the number of tricks (above 6) they commit to win, and the trump suit.

Trump suits: ♠ Spades, ♥ Hearts, ♦ Diamonds, ♣ Clubs.

Tricks: 13 tricks per hand. Highest card of led suit wins unless trumped.

Scoring: Make your contract = score points. Fall short = opponents score penalties.
