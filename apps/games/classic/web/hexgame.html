How to Play Hex

Goal: Connect your two opposite edges of the board with an unbroken chain of your stones.

Board: 11x11 rhombus of hexagonal cells.

Turns: Players alternate placing one stone on any empty hex. Black goes first.

Black wins by connecting the top edge to the bottom edge.

White wins by connecting the left edge to the right edge.

No draws: The board must always produce a winner -- it is mathematically impossible to fill the board without one player completing a connection.
