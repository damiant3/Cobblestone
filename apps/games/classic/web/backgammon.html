How to Play Backgammon

Goal: Move all 15 of your pieces off the board before your opponent.

Setup: 24 narrow triangles (points). Each player starts with 15 pieces in a standard arrangement.

Movement: Roll two dice. Move pieces forward by the number shown. Doubles mean four moves instead of two.

Hitting: Landing on a point with a single opponent piece sends it to the bar. It must re-enter before other pieces can move.

Bearing off: Once all your pieces are in your home board (last 6 points), you can start removing them.

Bar: Pieces on the bar must re-enter the board through the opponent's home board.

This simulation: AI plays both sides. Watch pieces move point to point with dice rolls shown.
