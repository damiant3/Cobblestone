How to Play the Royal Game of Ur

Goal: Be the first player to move all 7 pieces off the board.

Dice: Roll 4 binary dice (each 0 or 1) for a total of 0-4 pips.

Movement: Each piece travels along a fixed path. P1 (blue) enters from the top, P2 (red) from the bottom. Both share the middle row.

Rosettes (✦): Landing on a rosette grants an extra turn. Pieces on rosettes are safe from capture.

Capture: Landing on an opponent's piece in the shared row sends it back to the start.

Scoring: A piece that moves past the last square is scored. First to score all 7 wins.
