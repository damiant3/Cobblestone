How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe

Board: 3x3 grid. Players alternate placing X and O.

Goal: Be the first to get 3 of your marks in a row -- horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.

Draw: If all 9 cells are filled with no winner, it's a draw.

AI: Uses perfect-play minimax -- it will never lose.
