How to Play Monopoly

Goal: Be the last player with cash. All other players go bankrupt.

Flow: Roll dice, move around the 40-space board. Land on unowned property to buy it. Land on owned property to pay rent. Pass Go to collect $200.

Special spaces: Go (collect $200), Jail (just visiting or sent), Free Parking, Go To Jail (sent directly to Jail).

Elimination: If you cannot pay rent or taxes, you are bankrupt and eliminated.

This simulation: AI plays up to 200 turns. Last player standing wins.
