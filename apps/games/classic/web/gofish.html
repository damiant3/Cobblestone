How to Play Go Fish

Goal: Collect the most "books" (four cards of the same rank).

Play: On your turn, ask another player for a specific rank. If they have it, they hand it over. If not -- "Go Fish!" -- draw from the pond.

Books: When you collect all 4 cards of a rank, that's a book.

End: Game ends when all 13 books are completed. Most books wins.

Spectator mode: You can see every player's cards as the AI plays.
