Liar's Dice!
How to Play Liar's Dice

Goal: Be the last player with dice remaining.

Each round: All players secretly roll their dice. Players take turns bidding on the total number of a specific face value among ALL dice on the table.

Bidding: Each bid must raise the quantity or the face value. A player may challenge the previous bid by calling "Liar!"

Challenge: All dice are revealed. If the bid was too high, the bidder loses a die. If the bid was valid, the challenger loses a die.

End: When a player loses all their dice, they're eliminated. Last player standing wins.
