How to Play Battleship

Goal: Sink all of your opponent's ships before they sink yours.

Setup: Each fleet places 5 ships on a 10x10 grid. Ships cannot overlap.

Firing: Each turn, both fleets fire one salvo at a grid coordinate. A hit is marked with an explosion, a miss with a dot.

Ships: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), Destroyer (2) = 17 cells total.

Victory: First fleet to score 17 hits (sinking all enemy ships) wins.

This simulation: AI plays both fleets. Watch the grids fill with hits and misses until one fleet is destroyed.
