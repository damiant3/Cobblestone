How to Play Crazy Eights

Goal: Be the first player to empty your hand.

Play: On your turn, play a card that matches the rank or suit of the top discard card. If you can't play, draw from the pile.

Eights are wild: An 8 can be played on anything. The player who plays an 8 declares the new suit.

End: First player to play all their cards wins.
