How to Play Five Card Draw

Goal: Make the best five-card poker hand to beat your opponent.

The deal: Each player is dealt five cards face down from a standard 52-card deck.

The draw: After looking at their hand, each player may discard up to three cards (or four if holding an Ace) and receive replacements from the deck. This is the chance to improve your hand.

The showdown: After the draw, hands are revealed and compared. The player with the best poker hand wins.

Hand rankings (low to high): High Card, Pair, Two Pair, Three of a Kind, Straight, Flush, Full House, Four of a Kind, Straight Flush.

Strategy: Keep strong combinations (pairs, three of a kind) and discard weak cards hoping to draw better ones. Knowing when to hold and when to draw is the key skill.
