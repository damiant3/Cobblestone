# IoT Market Data -- Verified Claims

**Source**: Deep research workflow (107 agents, 25 sources, 20 confirmed,
5 adversarially refuted). All claims below survived 3-vote adversarial
verification unless noted.

## IoT MCU Market

- **2024**: $5.1B (contracted 9% YoY due to inventory correction)
- **2030 projections**: $7.32B (IoT Analytics, conservative) to $12.94B
  (Grand View Research). Wide variance reflects scope definitions.
- Corroborated by STMicroelectronics reporting 41% MCU sales drop and
  WSTS revising 2024 forecast from $27.7B to $23.2B.
- Source: IoT Analytics 91-page report (October 2025). Vote: 3-0.

## RISC-V Market

- **2025 valuations**: $1.11B (MarketsandMarkets) to $2.49B (Research
  and Markets). 2-3x spread reflects different scope (cores vs chips).
- **Growth**: 24.3% CAGR (M&M) to 41.23% CAGR (Mordor Intelligence)
- **IoT share**: ~34.5% of RISC-V revenue (confirmed by Mordor, SNS)
- **Asia-Pacific**: 42% of market (Mordor 42.33%, GM Insights 40.5%)
- **Driver**: Memory chip shortage accelerating adoption due to
  royalty-free licensing and modular design.
- Vote: 3-0 (across 5 merged claims).

## IoT Platform Market

- $44.30B (2025) to $65.29B (2030) at 8.1% CAGR (MarketsandMarkets)
- Massive cross-firm variance: Mordor $60.54B vs R&M $9.58B (6x spread)
- Leaders: Microsoft, AWS, Cisco, Huawei, Siemens
- Google Cloud IoT Core sunset August 2023 -- absent from leadership
- Vote: 3-0 (across 2 merged claims).

## IoT Vulnerability Landscape

- Devices with vulnerabilities: 14% (2023) to 33% (2024) = 136% YoY
- Based on Forescout analysis of ~19 million devices
- Forescout's Riskiest Connected Devices 2024 report
- Note: vendor research from customer base (selection bias possible)
- Vote: 3-0.

## Edge Computing

- Manufacturing: 22.58% of edge computing market (Mordor, 2025)
- IoT & Industrial Automation: largest application category (M&M)
- Healthcare: fastest-growing vertical (14.66% CAGR to 2031)
- Absolute market size could NOT be confirmed (both submitted
  estimates were adversarially refuted)
- Vote: 3-0 and 2-1 (across 2 merged claims).

## Caveats

Market sizing varies 2-6x across analyst firms for the same market.
All projections from commercial research firms with inherent modeling
uncertainty. The 2024 MCU contraction may bias near-term growth
projections.
